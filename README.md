# Backroom Billiards

8-ball pool for two players, in the browser. Play a friend online with a 4-character join code, play two at one PC, or practice alone.

Play it: https://jonbeam1-netizen.github.io/backroom-billiards/

- Mouse and keyboard, on a PC.
